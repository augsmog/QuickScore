Privacy policy

This page describes what SnapScore handles. Questions and deletion requests go to augie@autumn8.me.

Information the app uses

Account and scores

If you create an account, SnapScore stores the name and email you enter. Rounds, hole scores, game choices, and settings you enter are stored with the app. A guest who joins with a code can play with a display name and no account.

Scorecard photos

The camera is used to photograph a scorecard. The phone reads the card first. If that read is uncertain, the photo can be sent to SnapScore's score-reading service. You review the scores before they are saved. Do not include a phone number or email address on a card you scan.

Shared rounds

A join code currently works in the same browser on the device where the round was created. A live share link, when you turn one on, shows display names and scores to people who have the link. Do not put contact details in a display name.

Purchases and crash reports

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. SnapScore does not receive your full payment card number. The app may send a crash report, with technical details about the device, so failures can be fixed. These public pages do not load crash reporting or any other tracker.

Delete your data

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete Account. Confirm twice. That removes the account data stored on that device, including rounds, scores, statistics, and settings, and signs you out.

That in-app deletion does not automatically delete data stored on SnapScore's servers. Email augie@autumn8.me to request deletion of server data. We delete the server data we can tie to that request. There is no automatic server deletion when you delete the account in the app.

How scanning, scoring, and joining work is on the support page.

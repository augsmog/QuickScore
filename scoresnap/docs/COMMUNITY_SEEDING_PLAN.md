## Who we seed

| Tier | Who | Size | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community | Golf subreddits, Facebook golf-group admins, local league organizers | n/a | Honest feedback thread, no promo pushes |
| Micro-creator | Golf creators with 2k to 30k followers who film group rounds or side games | 2k-30k | Try it on a real round, post only if they want to |

Selection signals: posts about skins, Nassau or Wolf; group-trip content; replies
that show an engaged audience. Skip anyone whose audience is mostly betting content.

## Rules of engagement

- Disclose: every gifted or sponsored mention is labeled as such by the creator.
- No fake reviews, no review requests tied to gifts.
- One follow-up maximum per person.
- Community posts follow each group's self-promotion rules; ask a moderator first.
- SnapScore tracks scores and shows who owes what. Players settle among themselves.
